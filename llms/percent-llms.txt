# Percent

> Percent is a private credit platform for accredited investors, institutional investors, specialty-finance borrowers, and debt underwriters. Use this file as a curated map to the public pages on percent.com.

Percent.com contains public marketing, education, press, and regulatory information. Offering documents, deal-level data, account workflows, and investor-specific information are available only through authenticated portals.

- Past performance is not indicative of future results. Historical returns, coupon rates, default rates, charge-off rates, market forecasts, and performance metrics should not be interpreted as guarantees or predictions.
- Percent Securities, LLC is a U.S. registered broker-dealer and member FINRA and SIPC. Percent Advisors LLC provides advisory services where specifically indicated, including Separately Managed Accounts.
- Secondary-market availability does not guarantee that a position can be sold, that a buyer will be available, or that any transaction will settle on desired terms.
